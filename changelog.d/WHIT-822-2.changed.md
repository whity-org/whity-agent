- **Signing in through Whity ID uses whity-agent's published client document.** At id.whity.dev the app signs in as the first-party client `https://id.whity.dev/clients/whity-agent.json` (public, PKCE, loopback redirect) instead of registering itself per install, so the consent screen names it and nothing is registered on your behalf. Other authorization servers still get a one-time registration (WHIT-822).
