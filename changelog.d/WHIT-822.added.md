- **whity-agent, first version: sign in to a Whity workspace.** Type the workspace's address and sign in in your browser through its authorization server (Whity ID). The app uses OAuth with PKCE and a one-time loopback redirect, and registers itself once per authorization server. Tokens never reach the app's window: refresh tokens are kept only in your computer's keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or Linux Secret Service), and if there is no keychain the sign-in lasts until you close the app. English and Arabic, light and dark, with Whity's design tokens (WHIT-822).
